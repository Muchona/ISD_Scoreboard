ISD Form 8 Guide

Irish Citizenship 150-Point Rule Checklist (Identity & Residency Proofs)

When applying for Irish citizenship by naturalisation (Form 8), Immigration Service Delivery (ISD) uses a 150-point scorecard. You must reach 150 points for Identity and 150 points for Residency for every reckonable year claimed (5 years for most applicants, or 3 years for spouses/civil partners of an Irish citizen).

1. Proof of Identity (150 Points Required)

You only need to score 150 points once for your identity:

  • Current Valid In-Date Passport — 150 pts (or 75 pts if expiring within 6 months)
  • Irish Residence Permit (IRP) Card — supplementary identity verification
  • Expired Passport / Birth Certificate / National ID — combined to reach 150 pts when a valid passport is unavailable

2. Proof of Residency (150 Points Per Year)

For each reckonable year, combine 1 Type A (100 pts) + 1 Type B (50 pts), or 3 Type B documents (50 pts each):

CategoryPointsCommon Examples
Type A 100 pts Bank statements (showing regular day-to-day Irish transactions), Revenue Employment Detail Summary (EDS / P60), Mortgage statement, RTB/Tenancy agreement, Department of Social Protection letter
Type B 50 pts Utility bills (ESB, Bord Gáis, Electric Ireland), Phone/Broadband bills, Payslips, Private Health Insurance (VHI/Laya), GP/Hospital letters, Credit Card statements

Missing documents for a year? ISD allows an Affidavit (Form Citz-009) when standard 150-point proofs cannot be obtained.
