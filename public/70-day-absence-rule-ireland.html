Section 15C Continuous Residence

70-Day Absence Rule for Irish Citizenship (Final Year Guide)

In addition to reaching 1,825 (or 1,095) total reckonable days, you must have 1 year (365 days) of continuous residence in Ireland immediately before submitting your naturalisation application.

How the 70-Day Limit Works

  • Standard Allowance: Up to 70 days of total absences from Ireland during the 365 days immediately before your application date.
  • Exceptional Circumstances: Up to 30 additional days may be granted at the Minister's discretion for documented exceptional reasons (such as medical emergencies, family bereavement, or unavoidable work travel).
  • What if you are over 70 days? Each day you wait shifts your 365-day window forward — once older trips drop out of the last 365 days, your absence count drops automatically.
