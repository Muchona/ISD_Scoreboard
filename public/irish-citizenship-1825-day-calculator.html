Reckonable Residency

Irish Citizenship 1,825-Day & 1,095-Day Residency Calculator Guide

Before submitting Form 8 for Irish naturalisation, you must prove enough reckonable residence days from your Irish Residence Permit (IRP / GNIB) stamps, plus 1 year of continuous residence immediately prior to your application date.

General Rule vs. Spouse of an Irish Citizen

Applicant PathwayTotal Reckonable DaysLookback WindowFinal Year
Standard (Section 15) 1,825 days (5 years) Last 9 years 1 year continuous
Spouse / Civil Partner (Section 15A) 1,095 days (3 years) Last 5 years 1 year continuous

Which IRP Stamps Count?

  • Reckonable (Count): Stamp 1, Stamp 1H, Stamp 3, Stamp 4, Stamp 4S, Stamp 4EUFam, Stamp 5 (WCATT), and Stamp 1G (when held under qualifying graduate/partner conditions — check your official ISD permission letters).
  • Not Reckonable (Do Not Count): Stamp 2 / Stamp 2A (student permissions), Stamp 0, and periods without valid permission.
