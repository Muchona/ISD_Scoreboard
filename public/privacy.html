Zero-Upload Privacy

Privacy & 0 KB Document Upload Security

CitizenScore.ie was built so Irish citizenship applicants can verify their 150-point Form 8 proofs and watermark PDFs without ever sending their passport, IRP card, or bank statements over the internet.

1. 0 KB Uploaded — 100% In-Browser Processing

  • No file uploads: When you select a PDF or image in the Scorecard or PDF Watermarker, the file is read strictly inside your own device's browser memory (ArrayBuffer).
  • Local OCR & Watermarking: Smart document reading (PDF.js / Tesseract.js) and PDF watermarking (pdf-lib) execute completely on your phone or computer.
  • Test it yourself: After loading CitizenScore.ie, you can turn on Airplane Mode or disconnect Wi-Fi — document scanning and PDF watermarking still work 100%.

2. What Data Is Saved (Only If You Sign In)

  • Guest / Calculator Use: No account is required. Data stays in your browser's localStorage.
  • Signed-In Sync (EU GDPR): If you create an account, only your email address, residency stamp dates, absence dates, and scorecard checklist metadata (document category, points, and filename) are stored in EU-hosted Supabase servers — never your raw PDF or image files.
  • Payments & Analytics: Pro subscriptions are processed by Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1). Traffic stats use cookieless Vercel Web Analytics.

3. Your GDPR Rights & Contact

You can export or delete your account data at any time. For any privacy or security questions, email support@citizenscore.ie.
